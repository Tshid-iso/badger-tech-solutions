The leakage problem

Numbers get swapped, cash changes hands, and the trip — plus every trip after it — stops paying commission. The relationship you created becomes revenue you never see.

How it works

Consent first, then convenience — all of it inside your app.

The handshake is offered only after a premium rider completes a card-paid trip, so every saved pairing is already a paying, verified relationship.

The handshake

After a card-paid premium trip, a save prompt appears on the rider's side first, then the driver's. A pairing is created only when both opt in.

Saved Drivers

The driver lands in the rider's Saved Drivers list. Next time, the rider re-books that exact driver in a tap — no searching, no going elsewhere.

Rider retention

Escrow & filtered chat

Re-booked fares are held in escrow until the trip completes, and in-app chat screens out attempts to arrange off-platform payment. Your take rate stays whole.

What's in the SDK

Six parts, one job: stop the relationship from leaving.

Consent-based handshake

A two-sided opt-in — rider then driver — so no one is saved without agreement from both.

Saved Drivers list

Trusted drivers kept one tap away for premium riders, driving repeat on-platform bookings.

NLP payment-leak filters

In-app chat screens messages for attempts to share alternative payment details and blocks the hand-off.

Escrow holds

Saved-driver fares sit in escrow until completion, closing the cash-bypass window entirely.

Premium & card gating

The handshake unlocks only for subscribers who paid by card on that trip — quality by design.
